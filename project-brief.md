# Project brief

**Nigeria Malaria SNT Spatial Intelligence System**, GeoDev Lab Africa, Cohort 1.

**The question:** *Where in Nigeria does subnational tailoring actually assign
different malaria intervention mixes to neighbouring LGAs?* The study area is
all 36 states and the FCT, analysed at Local Government Area level (774 LGAs),
because the LGA is the unit at which the National Malaria Strategic Plan
assigns intervention packages.

**The data:** the LGA intervention mix from Annex 1 of the NMSP 2021–2025, the
OCHA COD administrative boundaries and 2020 population estimates, and, from
Month 3, the GRID3 health facility points. Every source, with its link, is in
[`data-notes.md`](data-notes.md).

**The answer so far:** 530 of the 2,210 shared LGA borders separate different
intervention mixes. See [The answer](README.md#the-answer) in the main README.

The full Week 1 brief, with the data needs, the feasibility check and the
deferred questions, is in [`docs/01-project-brief.md`](docs/01-project-brief.md).
It stays there because Month 1 work is not moved.
